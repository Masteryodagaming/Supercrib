SYMPTOM
- RTDB talking/{seat} works (Opp / "Voice not linked" reacts), but WebRTC never
  reaches connectionState === 'connected'. Common on phone/cellular/symmetric NAT
  with STUN-only ICE — candidates never form a path, peer stays not-linked forever.

FIXES (src/multiplayer/voicePtt.ts)

1) TURN (not STUN-only)
- Export VOICE_ICE_SERVERS: Google STUN + free OpenRelay (metered.ca openrelayproject)
  turn:80, turn:443, turn:443?transport=tcp — no paid Twilio.
- RTCPeerConnection({ iceServers: VOICE_ICE_SERVERS }).
- VOICE_STUN_SERVERS kept as STUN-only filter alias for compat/tests.

2) Buffer remote ICE until remote description set
- pendingRemoteIce queue; addIceCandidate only after setRemoteDescription;
  flush after offer/answer applied. Prevents lost candidates → permanent not-linked.

3) Treat ICE connected as live
- onconnectionstatechange + oniceconnectionstatechange
- status 'live' when connectionState === 'connected' OR iceConnectionState
  connected/completed (Safari often lags connectionState).

4) ICE restart on failed
- Host once: createOffer({ iceRestart: true }), clear own ice/{seat} only,
  guest re-answers. UI resets to connecting (not stuck on error).

5) Negotiation races
- Host-only offers; guest re-answers new offers while stable (mic replaceTrack /
  ICE restart). Own-seat ICE wipe only — never wipe the other seat’s candidates.

6) UI
- Keep “Voice not linked” when remoteTalking && !peerLive (no Opp until live).
- Brief “Connecting voice…” while status is connecting/ready and !peerLive.
- Table/emoji rails unchanged.
