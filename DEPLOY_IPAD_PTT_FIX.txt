WHY THE MIC WAS MISSING
1) Mount gate — App only passed `voicePtt` when `session.connection === 'connected'`.
   During an active online match, presence can briefly report waiting/disconnected, so
   the Hold mic never mounted even though play was live.
2) Clip — `.table-mid-zone { overflow: hidden }` + flex `min-height: 0` clipped
   `.ptt-wrap` at the bottom of the mid-zone when height was tight (esp. iPad
   landscape under the @900px “desktop” styles).

WHY THE TABLE LOOKED CRUSHED ON iPAD
- `@media (min-width: 900px)` treats iPad as desktop: framed shell + large
  `--board-w: clamp(260px, 30vw, 400px)` on a short landscape viewport.
- `.board-wood-plank { min-height: 0 }` let WebKit flex-shrink the plank below its
  `aspect-ratio: 280/400`, so Arena (`background-size: 100% 100%`) looked
  vertically squashed. Mid-zone overflow then hid scores/PTT.

FIXES

App.tsx
- Pass `voicePtt` whenever online + not spectating.
- Add `voiceReady: session.connection === 'connected'`.
- Voice WebRTC wiring still gated on `bothConnected` in useVoicePtt (unchanged).

Table.tsx
- Move PushToTalkButton OUT of `.table-mid-zone` → sibling `.table-ptt-slot`
  between mid-zone and hand (above hand, clear of rails/avatars/ZOOM/scores).
- Show mic whenever `voicePtt` provided; `disabled` + “Connecting…” when
  `voiceReady === false`. Still hidden for vs-AI / spectate / gameOver.

PushToTalkButton.tsx
- `notReadyHint` when disabled (short Connecting… line under the pill).

styles.css
- Plank: `min-height: calc(var(--board-w) * 400 / 280)` + pegboard `min-height: auto`
  so flex cannot squash aspect-ratio (hole math / paint untouched).
- `.table-ptt-slot` flex-none, z-index 22, above hand.
- Tablet overrides AFTER desktop @900/@1100:
    @media (min-width: 900px) and (hover: none),
           (min-width: 900px) and (max-width: 1100px)
      → phone-like `--board-w: clamp(190px, 24vw, 230px)`, flush side avatars,
        readable hand, reaction docks aligned, no weird letterboxing shell.
    @media (min-width: 900px) and (max-height: 820px)
      → height-aware board clamp for landscape iPad / short windows.
- Real mouse desktops (hover:hover, tall) keep framed @900/@1100 shell.
