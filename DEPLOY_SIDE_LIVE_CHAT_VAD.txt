What shipped (preview)
----------------------
1. Side button **"Join live chat"** on the left play rail (not the old mid-zone
   Hold PTT control).
2. After Join: status **"Live"** with **green** indicator when WebRTC is live;
   **red** when joining / disconnected / failed / not linked.
3. **Voice-activated (VAD)** once in — mic transmits while speaking, muted when
   quiet. No hold-to-talk required. Leave returns to Join.
4. Join click is the Safari/iPad mic + remote-audio unlock gesture.
5. Online multiplayer only (room peers); reuses existing WebRTC + RTDB voice
   signaling (rooms/{code}/voice/).
