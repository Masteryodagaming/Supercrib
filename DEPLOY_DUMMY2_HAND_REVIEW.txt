  review step vanished before Josh could act for Dummy2.

Fix
---
1. vs-AI: human always sees Approve/Adjust on pendingReview (Dummy2 review

3. Copy in review / count-approval UI: claim → hand
   - "Approve hand (N)" / "Adjust hand"

4. Online seat gating unchanged (only reviewSeat === viewer gets buttons).
