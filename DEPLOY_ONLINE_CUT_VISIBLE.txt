Root cause
----------
1) Starter cut (Table): seat gating only showed CutFan when
   `pone === viewer` (or AI anim). Dealer waiting for pone got banner
   "Waiting for … to cut…" with NO deck fan and NO reveal. When RTDB applied
   cutStarter, phase jumped straight to pegging — spectator never saw the cut.
   Prior Dummy2 cut timing was AI-only and did not cover online sync.

2) Cut-for-deal (CutForDeal): online non-actor got text placeholder
   "Opponent is cutting — hang tight…" instead of a visible deck fan.

Fix
---
1) Waiting starter cut: show non-interactive CutFan (watchMode) for dealer /
   spectator while phase === 'cut' and they are not the cutter.
2) Online remote reveal: when spectator locally saw phase 'cut' then RTDB
   advances past cut with a starter, replay local select→reveal anim
   (aiCutAnim.remote) without calling cutStarter again; UI stays locked until
   reveal finishes. Works both directions (whoever is not pone).
3) Cut-for-deal online: show disabled DealCutFan while waiting (both seats),
   not a blank placeholder.
4) Helpers + unit tests: onlineCutWatch.ts (waiting fan + remote reveal edge).
