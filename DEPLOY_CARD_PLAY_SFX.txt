Josh: Playing a card sounds like a coin dropping in a bucket. Wants a card
sound instead. Preview ONLY. Do not promote live. Do not touch voice/chat.

Root cause
----------
On every peg sequence growth, App.tsx called BOTH playCard() AND playPeg(points).
Even non-scoring plays layered peg wood WAVs (dice clacks that read metallic /
coin-in-bucket) on top of the card slap. Scoring plays also layered prize synth.

Fix
---
1. App.tsx — plain card play: playCard() only (card_play1/2.wav).
   playPeg(pts) only when this play scored points (pts > 0) → wood tick +
   prize tiers (2/4/6/12) for scoring pegs only.
2. gameSfx.ts — synthCard fallback = quiet cardboard (soft mid rustle + low
   thump); no bright highpass / metallic plink.
3. Peg sample order prefers peg_wood.wav (woody) ahead of peg1/2/3.
4. Kept: shuffle WAVs; withRunningCtx / listeners-until-running unlock;
   Settings mute; go/win synth; VOICE_PTT_ENABLED = false.
