Josh (two phones, mobile Live): can hear host; host cannot hear Josh.

Root cause
----------
1) Guest (often seat1) answered the host offer during start() BEFORE
   adoptMicStream attached the mic → SDP a=recvonly. Host→guest media
   worked; guest→host never got a send m-line. Renegotiate-after-mic was
   unreliable once ICE was already live one-way on Safari.

2) createOffer/createAnswer with sender track.enabled=false could emit
   a=inactive/recvonly on Safari even after replaceTrack.

3) ensureMic (fallback path) attached the raw capture track without the
   VAD monitor/sender clone — same silent-analyser deadlock as before on
   any seat that hit that path.

4) Host remote <audio> could stay on a silent first attach; remote track
   unmute did not retry play().

Fix
---
- shouldAnswerOfferWithoutMic() → false; queue offer until micAttached;
  flush after adoptMicStream/ensureMic (Join already has gesture mic).
- Briefly enable sender clone around createOffer/createAnswer, then
  restore VAD gate (capture/restoreSendTrackEnabledForSdp).
- forceAudioSendRecv() after mic attach + on setTalking(true) + on Live
  (ensureOutboundAudio one-shot renegotiate bump for answerer).
- ensureMic uses cloneMicTrackForVad (same as adoptMicStream).
- Accumulate remote tracks into one MediaStream across renegotiate.
- remoteVoicePlayback: track.onunmute → re-enable + play().
- Preserve-mic remount keeps monitor track.enabled=true for VAD.
