Silence fix (what broke)
------------------------
bindUnlockOnce removed pointer/key/touch listeners after the FIRST gesture even
when resume() had not finished / ctx still suspended. Mount-time
unlockGameAudio() in useEffect([]) has no gesture on Chrome — useless alone.
play* could schedule WAV/synth while ctx.state === 'suspended' → silent.

Fix
---
- Keep gesture listeners (pointerdown/up, click, keydown, touchstart/end) until
  ctx.state === 'running'; re-bind if ctx later suspends.
- withRunningCtx: resume().then(play) so shuffle/deal/card/peg/go/win wait for
  running before starting buffers/oscillators.
- Settings sound still gated (default on); mute immediate via setSoundEnabled.
