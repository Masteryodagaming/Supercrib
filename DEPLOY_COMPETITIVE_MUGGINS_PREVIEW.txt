------------------------------------------

- Type the **raw** hand/crib count on the keypad (not the ×2 board total).
- Non-spade starter: badge still shows ×2 show — after exact raw claim,
  board pegs raw then ×2 bonus (same net as Normal).
- Spade starter: claim raw; badge spade · raw; no ×2 bonus.
- Overclaim: board snaps to true raw (+×2), then −(claim − raw).
- Underclaim: claimed raw pegs (no ×2); Accept leaves miss, or Muggins
